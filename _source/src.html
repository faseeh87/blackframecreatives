How a project runs

Brief to launch
in four frames

A typical project takes two to four weeks. You always know what happens next and who's doing it.

  1. FRAME 01

    Discovery call

    A free call about your goals, audience and budget. Then you get a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

    Week 1
  2. FRAME 02

    Plan & shot list

    We map every image to where it will be used, then build the shot list, mood board, locations and schedule.

    Week 1–2
  3. FRAME 03

    Shoot day

    Lighting, styling and direction on set. You can join, approve frames live on a tablet, or leave it with us.

    Week 2
  4. FRAME 04

    Grade & launch

    Edited, colour-graded files plus the posts, ads or pages they were made for, scheduled and ready to go live.

    Week 3–4

Before you ask

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask on the form below or message us on WhatsApp.

Can we use the photos anywhere?

Yes. Edited images are delivered for your brand's web, social, print and ad use, and the exact usage terms are written into your proposal before we start.

How quickly do we get the edits?

Turnaround is agreed in your proposal and depends on the size of the shoot. For events and launches we can deliver a quick highlight set for social while the buzz is still live.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Absolutely. We can hand over shot lists, edited files and ad creative to your team, or take the whole campaign on ourselves. Whatever fills the gap.

Do you travel outside Mangalore?

Yes. We shoot across Mangalore and are happy to travel further for the right project. Any travel costs are quoted up front.

Is ad spend included in the packages?

No. Package prices cover our work. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google, and we'll recommend a budget that fits your goals.
